# Reference screens: Reports and Settings (from the owner's screenshots)

Source: 50 screenshots of a reference product's Reports screens (Desktop\reports) and 25 of its Settings screens (Desktop\settimgs), read one by one on 2026-11-10. This file records layout, button placement, window format and what each feature is for, so our Reports and Settings can match the organisation and the working. No personal data from the screenshots is kept. Branding is not copied.

## A. Reports: shell and common patterns (screens 365 to 382)

Shell
- Left rail with the module menu; "Reports" opens a second column (a sub-menu, about 270 px) of report names grouped under grey section headings: Transaction report, Party report, GST reports, and more below (read from later screenshots). The chosen report is highlighted in the sub-menu with a left accent bar. The main area fills the rest.
- Sections so far: Transaction report (Sale, Purchase, Day book, All Transactions, Profit And Loss, Bill Wise Profit, Cash flow, Trial Balance Report, Balance Sheet); Party report (Party Statement, Party wise Profit & Loss, All parties, Party Report By Item, Sale Purchase By Party, Sale Purchase By Party Group); GST reports (GSTR 1, GSTR 2, GSTR 3B, GSTR 9, Sale Summary By HSN, SAC Report, and more below).

Common header (list-style reports)
- Period picker at top left: a large "This Month" drop-down (presets), then a segmented "Between [from] To [to]" box. A firm selector ("All Firms") sits beside it. A second row holds report-specific filters (transaction type, party, category, item) as plain selects.
- Top right: two labelled icon buttons, "Excel Report" and "Print". Statement-type reports (P&L, Trial Balance, Balance Sheet) use round icon buttons (Excel, Print or PDF and XLS) instead.
- Below the header: a search box at left, then the table.
- Table: column headers in small caps, each with a funnel icon that opens a per-column filter; a "#" serial column; the last column "Print / Share" with a print icon, a share icon and a kebab menu per row. Numbers right-aligned with the rupee sign.
- Footer bar pinned to the bottom of the card with totals in coloured text (green for in or receivable, red for out or payable) laid out left, middle and right.
- Empty state: centred "No transactions to show".

Individual reports

## B. Settings (screens 459 to 483)

Shell
- Settings opens as a full-window layer over the app. A dark navy rail at the left (about 320 px) with the title "Settings" and a search icon at the top, then ten tabs in this order: General, Transaction, Print, Taxes & GST, Transaction Message, Party, Item, Service Reminders, Accounting, Multi Currency. The open tab is a white pill that runs flush to the content. A round close button sits at the top right of the content.
- Content is a light grey page with three columns of short sections. Each section has a bold heading with a thin rule under it. Most settings are a check box with a label and a small grey "i" icon (hover help). A setting that needs a value shows the value beside the label (a number box, a drop-down). Sub-options are indented under their parent and appear only when the parent is on.
- Settings save the moment they are changed; there is no Save button except where a section has "Apply" (screen zoom).

## C. What our product has against these screens (status as of 2026-11-10)

Reports (shell built: the report list on the left, shared period bar, searchable table with column filters, totals footer, Excel (CSV) and Print)
- Built or already present and now inside the shell: Sale (sales register), Purchase and All transactions (new list), Day book (new), Profit and loss, Bill wise profit (new, real cost from the stock ledger), Cash flow, Trial balance, Balance sheet, cash/bank/expense books, receivables and payables by party, party wise profit and loss, GSTR-1/3B, GSTR-2B/2A, GSTR-9/9C, GST rate report (new), stock summary, item and style profit, stock ageing and movement, colour and size, discounts, TCS, TDS, MSME, sale orders, backorders.
- Not built (no screen yet): Party statement as a standalone list, All parties with credit limit, Party report by item, Sale/Purchase by party and by party group, Sale summary by HSN and SAC report as lists (the data is in the GSTR-1 JSON), Low stock summary list, Stock detail (opening, in, out, closing per item), Item detail (day by day), Sale/Purchase by item category, Stock summary by item category, Item wise discount list, Bank statement report, Discount report, Form 27EQ, TCS receivable, TDS payable and receivable as lists, Expense category and expense item reports, Sale order item report, Loan statement and loan accounts.
- Differences on purpose: GSTR forms stay in the GST area; balance-sheet horizontal/vertical toggle and the trial-balance "working" option are not built; the Excel button saves a CSV that Excel opens.

Settings (shell built: tabs on a rail with search; each tab shows the settings that live there as controls or as links to the screen where they are kept)
- Controls built and working: Print (what the invoice header, totals and footer show, paper A4 or A5, portrait or landscape, colour; read by the printed invoice and quotation; live sample), Screen size (device only).
- Everything else on the tabs is a link to a screen that already exists (company, financial years, godowns, users, controls, backup, audit trail, import, devices, number series, approvals, locks, POS, expenses, GST, HSN, TCS and TDS, message templates, party and price lists, loyalty, items, margins, stock limits, repairs, chart of accounts, ledger defaults, closing).
- Not built because the product has no feature behind them yet, so no control is shown: passcode, decimal places, stop sale on negative stock, block new items or parties from a transaction form, estimate/proforma/order/delivery-challan switches, round off total, billing type, transaction-wise tax and discount, quick entry, repeat invoices, payment-term fields, additional party fields, serial/batch/model/size item fields and custom fields, thermal printer layouts (five themes, page widths, cut and drawer), regular printer themes (Tally, landscape, GST) beyond the colour, message sending options (WhatsApp login, auto-send per document type), service reminders switch, multi-currency. Each needs its behaviour built first.

### Update, later on 10 Nov 2026
Reports now built on the shared list page (/reports/list/<key>): party statement, all parties with credit limit, party report by item, sale and purchase by party and by party group, sale summary by HSN, SAC report, low stock, stock detail, item detail, item wise discount, expense category and expense item reports, sale order items, loan statement (loan ledgers: drawn, repaid, balance due), plus two the brief asked for: stock classes A, B, C (how often to cycle count) and customers due to order again. Still not built, each needing its feature first: decimal places for amounts and quantities, passcode for edit and delete (the product already asks for the password again on risky actions), thermal printer layouts, regular printer themes beyond colour, automatic WhatsApp message per document type (the message templates and sending exist under CRM; sending a sale invoice automatically on creation does not, only counter bills can), multi-currency.
