# INV-022 Stock counts

Design specification. Baseline status from the discovery: **FULLY IMPLEMENTED** (Built: the design below restyles and extends a working feature.)

1. **Feature ID**: INV-022
2. **Feature name**: Stock counts
3. **Parent module**: INV Inventory and Masters of Stock
4. **Business purpose**: Count a store by scanning barcodes. An approver sees the differences and their value, and approving puts the books right. (screen text of `/inventory/counts/[id]`)
5. **Primary persona**: accountant, sales, purchase, storekeeper (roles holding its permissions, `07_ROLES_AND_PERMISSION_MATRIX`); owner and admin hold all.
6. **Entry point**: Inventory > counting > `/inventory/counts`; Transactions > accounts > `/accounts/bill-receipt`; Finance > books > `/accounts/bill-receipt`; Finance > banking > `/accounts/reconciliation`; GST and Compliance > tax > `/accounts/tds`; Finance > planning > `/accounts/working-capital`; Inventory > counti
7. **Related screens**: `/inventory/counts/[id]` (Stock counts), `/inventory/counts` (Stock counts), `/print/count-sheet/[id]` (Count sheet)
8. **Subfeatures** (one per route, from the discovery):
   - `GET /stock-counts` (apps/api/src/routes/stockcount.ts:17)
   - `GET /stock-counts/scopes` (apps/api/src/routes/stockcount.ts:22)
   - `POST /stock-counts` (apps/api/src/routes/stockcount.ts:23)
   - `GET /stock-counts/:id` (apps/api/src/routes/stockcount.ts:30)
   - `POST /stock-counts/:id/scan` (apps/api/src/routes/stockcount.ts:32)
   - `POST /stock-counts/:id/import-scans` (apps/api/src/routes/stockcount.ts:37)
   - `PUT /stock-counts/:id/lines` (apps/api/src/routes/stockcount.ts:41)
   - `POST /stock-counts/:id/reset` (apps/api/src/routes/stockcount.ts:46)
   - `POST /stock-counts/:id/submit` (apps/api/src/routes/stockcount.ts:47)
   - `POST /stock-counts/:id/reopen` (apps/api/src/routes/stockcount.ts:51)
   - `POST /stock-counts/:id/approve` (apps/api/src/routes/stockcount.ts:55)
   - `POST /stock-counts/:id/cancel` (apps/api/src/routes/stockcount.ts:59)
9. **Screen layout**: pattern **Approval and workflow** (`06_SHARED_COMPONENTS`). Desktop: page header (title, description, primary action) over a queue on the left and the selected request on the right, with the stage line and the decision actions in the footer. [proposed layout]
10. **Component hierarchy** [proposed]: `PageHeader` > `FilterBar` > `DataTable` (or `Editor`/`ObjectHeader` + `Tabs`) > `StatusBadge`, `EmptyState`, `ErrorState`, `LoadingSkeleton`; dialogs through `ConfirmDialog`.
11. **Fields** (labels the current screens carry): Warehouse, What to count, Name, Scan a barcode, Reason for the differences.
12. **Columns** (current screens): Item, Barcode, In the books, Counted, Difference, Value, Count, Name, Warehouse, Started, Pieces counted, Stage Default view shows the first 6 to 8; the rest sit behind the column chooser [proposed].
13. **Filters** [proposed from the data]: search, status, scope (store, branch) where the permission is scoped.
14. **Primary actions**: `POST /stock-counts`.
15. **Secondary actions**: `POST /stock-counts/:id/scan`, `POST /stock-counts/:id/import-scans`, `PUT /stock-counts/:id/lines`, `POST /stock-counts/:id/reset`, `POST /stock-counts/:id/submit`, `POST /stock-counts/:id/reopen`, `POST /stock-counts/:id/approve`, `POST /stock-counts/:id/cancel`.
16. **Contextual actions** [proposed]: open the linked document, copy number, view audit history, and row actions limited to what the status and permissions allow.
17. **Validation and constraints**: the server remains the authority (`zod` schemas in the route file, domain rules in `packages/domain`); the form mirrors them inline and never relaxes them. Lifecycle `stockCount` allows only: counting -> submitted, cancelled; submitted -> approved, counting, cancelled; approved -> final; cancelled -> final.
18. **Approval behaviour**: `POST /stock-counts/:id/approve`. Show who must decide, why the action is blocked, and the history.
19. **Permissions**: `inventory.approve`, `inventory.cancel`, `inventory.create`, `inventory.edit`, `inventory.view`. Menu entries and buttons are hidden without permission; the API refuses regardless.
20. **Business status states**: counting > submitted > approved > cancelled; each shown as a status badge with icon and text (never colour alone).
21. **Loading states**: skeleton rows or blocks of the final shape; actions disabled while saving to stop duplicate submission.
22. **Empty states**: say what the screen is for and offer the primary action (or, for a report, widen the period).
23. **Error states**: show the server's reason in plain words and what to do; keep the user's input; permission failures say which permission is missing.
24. **Responsive behaviour** [proposed]: desktop 1280 and above full layout; tablet 768 collapses the object-page tabs into a scroll row and the grid to key columns; mobile 375 uses stacked records with a bottom action bar. Tables never shrink; they scroll or restack.
25. **Keyboard interactions**: Tab order follows reading order; Enter submits the focused form; Esc closes dialogs; list rows open with Enter. Do not rebind browser shortcuts.
26. **Offline behaviour**: `stock.count` is **allowed**: Count stock by scanning, as a spot check..
27. **API dependencies**: `GET /stock-counts`, `GET /stock-counts/scopes`, `POST /stock-counts`, `GET /stock-counts/:id`, `POST /stock-counts/:id/scan`, `POST /stock-counts/:id/import-scans`, `PUT /stock-counts/:id/lines`, `POST /stock-counts/:id/reset` and 4 more. Contracts are not changed by the redesign.
28. **Existing backend limitations**: Routes, a screen and automated tests that call the routes are all present. Open items for this module are in `docs/product-discovery/10_IMPLEMENTATION_STATUS.md` section C.
29. **Related reports**: INV-001 Reports: branch comparison, INV-009 Reports: stock summary, INV-010 Reports: item ledger, INV-011 Reports: reorder, INV-014 Reports: stock losses
30. **Acceptance criteria**:
    - Every route listed under subfeatures still has an entry point.
    - Permissions match section 19 on screen and on the API; a user without them cannot reach the feature by menu, search or link.
    - Loading, empty, error and permission-denied states exist and were looked at.
    - No control is decorative: each one performs its action or is absent.
    - Keyboard-only use completes the primary task; focus is visible and returns sensibly after a dialog.
    - Layout verified at 1440, 1280, 768 and 375 px; tables scroll or restack.
    - Test evidence: `demo-pack.test.ts`, `lifecycle.test.ts`, `scanimport.test.ts` still pass.
    - Light and dark themes both checked; status is never shown by colour alone.
