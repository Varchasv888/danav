# EXP-002 Expenses

Design specification. Baseline status from the discovery: **FULLY IMPLEMENTED** (Built: the design below restyles and extends a working feature.)

1. **Feature ID**: EXP-002
2. **Feature name**: Expenses
3. **Parent module**: EXP Expenses
4. **Business purpose**: Staff claim what they spent; approving books it (with GST credit where allowed) and paying settles it. (screen text of `/expenses/new`)
5. **Primary persona**: accountant, hr (roles holding its permissions, `07_ROLES_AND_PERMISSION_MATRIX`); owner and admin hold all.
6. **Entry point**: Finance > assetsExpenses > `/expenses`; Masters > orgFinance > `/settings/expenses`; Finance > assetsExpenses > `/expenses/travel`
7. **Related screens**: `/expenses/[id]/edit` (Expense claim), `/expenses/[id]` (Expense claim), `/expenses/new` (Expense claims), `/expenses` (Expense claims), `/settings/expenses` (Expense categories)
8. **Subfeatures** (one per route, from the discovery):
   - `GET /expenses/people` (apps/api/src/routes/expenses.ts:38)
   - `GET /expenses/categories` (apps/api/src/routes/expenses.ts:48)
   - `POST /expenses/categories` (apps/api/src/routes/expenses.ts:49)
   - `PUT /expenses/categories/:id` (apps/api/src/routes/expenses.ts:50)
   - `GET /expenses/claims` (apps/api/src/routes/expenses.ts:67)
   - `GET /expenses/totals` (apps/api/src/routes/expenses.ts:71)
   - `GET /expenses/claims/:id` (apps/api/src/routes/expenses.ts:72)
   - `POST /expenses/claims` (apps/api/src/routes/expenses.ts:73)
   - `PUT /expenses/claims/:id` (apps/api/src/routes/expenses.ts:74)
   - `POST /expenses/claims/:id/submit` (apps/api/src/routes/expenses.ts:75)
   - `POST /expenses/claims/:id/approve` (apps/api/src/routes/expenses.ts:76)
   - `POST /expenses/claims/:id/reject` (apps/api/src/routes/expenses.ts:77)
   - `POST /expenses/claims/:id/pay` (apps/api/src/routes/expenses.ts:82)
   - `POST /expenses/claims/:id/cancel` (apps/api/src/routes/expenses.ts:87)
9. **Screen layout**: pattern **Approval and workflow** (`06_SHARED_COMPONENTS`). Desktop: page header (title, description, primary action) over a queue on the left and the selected request on the right, with the stage line and the decision actions in the footer. [proposed layout]
10. **Component hierarchy** [proposed]: `PageHeader` > `FilterBar` > `DataTable` (or `Editor`/`ObjectHeader` + `Tabs`) > `StatusBadge`, `EmptyState`, `ErrorState`, `LoadingSkeleton`; dialogs through `ConfirmDialog`.
11. **Fields** (labels the current screens carry): Employee, Date, Purpose, Project, Category, From, To, Kilometres, Days away, Amount (₹), GST %, Details, Vendor, Vendor GSTIN.
12. **Columns** (current screens): Date, Category, Vendor, GST credit, Amount, Claim, Employee, Purpose, Status Default view shows the first 6 to 8; the rest sit behind the column chooser [proposed].
13. **Filters** [proposed from the data]: search, date range, status, scope (store, branch) where the permission is scoped.
14. **Primary actions**: none (read only).
15. **Secondary actions**: `POST /expenses/categories`, `PUT /expenses/categories/:id`, `POST /expenses/claims`, `PUT /expenses/claims/:id`, `POST /expenses/claims/:id/submit`, `POST /expenses/claims/:id/approve`, `POST /expenses/claims/:id/reject`, `POST /expenses/claims/:id/pay`.
16. **Contextual actions** [proposed]: open the linked document, copy number, view audit history, and row actions limited to what the status and permissions allow.
17. **Validation and constraints**: the server remains the authority (`zod` schemas in the route file, domain rules in `packages/domain`); the form mirrors them inline and never relaxes them. No written lifecycle applies.
18. **Approval behaviour**: `POST /expenses/claims/:id/approve`, `POST /expenses/claims/:id/reject`. Show who must decide, why the action is blocked, and the history.
19. **Permissions**: `expenses.approve`, `expenses.cancel`, `expenses.create`, `expenses.edit`, `expenses.submit`, `expenses.view`. Menu entries and buttons are hidden without permission; the API refuses regardless.
20. **Business status states**: no lifecycle states are documented for this feature.
21. **Loading states**: skeleton rows or blocks of the final shape; actions disabled while saving to stop duplicate submission.
22. **Empty states**: say what the screen is for and offer the primary action (or, for a report, widen the period).
23. **Error states**: show the server's reason in plain words and what to do; keep the user's input; permission failures say which permission is missing.
24. **Responsive behaviour** [proposed]: desktop 1280 and above full layout; tablet 768 collapses the object-page tabs into a scroll row and the grid to key columns; mobile 375 uses stacked records with a bottom action bar. Tables never shrink; they scroll or restack.
25. **Keyboard interactions**: Tab order follows reading order; Enter submits the focused form; Esc closes dialogs; list rows open with Enter. Do not rebind browser shortcuts.
26. **Offline behaviour**: `expense.claim` is **allowed**: Submit an expense claim..
27. **API dependencies**: `GET /expenses/people`, `GET /expenses/categories`, `POST /expenses/categories`, `PUT /expenses/categories/:id`, `GET /expenses/claims`, `GET /expenses/totals`, `GET /expenses/claims/:id`, `POST /expenses/claims` and 6 more. Contracts are not changed by the redesign.
28. **Existing backend limitations**: Routes, a screen and automated tests that call the routes are all present. Open items for this module are in `docs/product-discovery/10_IMPLEMENTATION_STATUS.md` section C.
29. **Related reports**: EXP-003 Reports: travel claims
30. **Acceptance criteria**:
    - Every route listed under subfeatures still has an entry point.
    - Permissions match section 19 on screen and on the API; a user without them cannot reach the feature by menu, search or link.
    - Loading, empty, error and permission-denied states exist and were looked at.
    - No control is decorative: each one performs its action or is absent.
    - Keyboard-only use completes the primary task; focus is visible and returns sensibly after a dialog.
    - Layout verified at 1440, 1280, 768 and 375 px; tables scroll or restack.
    - Test evidence: `expenses-projects.test.ts`, `travelclaims.test.ts` still pass.
    - Light and dark themes both checked; status is never shown by colour alone.
