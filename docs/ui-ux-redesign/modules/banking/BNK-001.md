# BNK-001 Banking: accounts

Design specification. Baseline status from the discovery: **FULLY IMPLEMENTED** (Built: the design below restyles and extends a working feature.)

1. **Feature ID**: BNK-001
2. **Feature name**: Banking: accounts
3. **Parent module**: BNK Banking
4. **Business purpose**: Match your bank statements with the books. (screen text of `/banking`)
5. **Primary persona**: accountant (roles holding its permissions, `07_ROLES_AND_PERMISSION_MATRIX`); owner and admin hold all.
6. **Entry point**: Masters > orgFinance > `/banking`; Finance > banking > `/banking`; Finance > banking > `/banking/rules`; Finance > banking > `/banking/settlements`; Finance > banking > `/accounts/reconciliation`
7. **Related screens**: `/banking/[id]`, `/banking` (Banks), `/banking/rules` (Bank rules), `/banking/settlements` (Gateway settlements)
8. **Subfeatures** (one per route, from the discovery):
   - `GET /banking/accounts` (apps/api/src/routes/banking.ts:23)
   - `PUT /banking/accounts/:id/profile` (apps/api/src/routes/banking.ts:24)
   - `POST /banking/accounts/:id/statements` (apps/api/src/routes/banking.ts:29)
   - `POST /banking/accounts/:id/feed/connect` (apps/api/src/routes/banking.ts:33)
   - `POST /banking/accounts/:id/feed/disconnect` (apps/api/src/routes/banking.ts:34)
   - `POST /banking/accounts/:id/feed/fetch` (apps/api/src/routes/banking.ts:35)
   - `GET /banking/accounts/:id/lines` (apps/api/src/routes/banking.ts:39)
   - `GET /banking/accounts/:id/open-entries` (apps/api/src/routes/banking.ts:43)
   - `POST /banking/accounts/:id/apply-rules` (apps/api/src/routes/banking.ts:47)
   - `GET /banking/accounts/:id/reconciliation` (apps/api/src/routes/banking.ts:48)
9. **Screen layout**: pattern **Master or record (list, form, object detail)** (`06_SHARED_COMPONENTS`). Desktop: page header (title, description, primary action) over a register (search, saved views, column chooser) and an object page for one record with tabs and an activity timeline. [proposed layout]
10. **Component hierarchy** [proposed]: `PageHeader` > `FilterBar` > `DataTable` (or `Editor`/`ObjectHeader` + `Tabs`) > `StatusBadge`, `EmptyState`, `ErrorState`, `LoadingSkeleton`; dialogs through `ConfirmDialog`.
11. **Fields** (labels the current screens carry): As on, Post to ledger, Narration, Bank, Account (last 4 digits), IFSC, Narration contains, Money, Post to, Priority, Gateway, Paid into, Collections were booked to, In bank.
12. **Columns** (current screens): Settled, Settlement, Payments, Collected, Refunds, Fees, GST on fees, Paid out, In bank Default view shows the first 6 to 8; the rest sit behind the column chooser [proposed].
13. **Filters** [proposed from the data]: search, scope (store, branch) where the permission is scoped.
14. **Primary actions**: none (read only).
15. **Secondary actions**: `PUT /banking/accounts/:id/profile`, `POST /banking/accounts/:id/statements`, `POST /banking/accounts/:id/feed/connect`, `POST /banking/accounts/:id/feed/disconnect`, `POST /banking/accounts/:id/feed/fetch`, `POST /banking/accounts/:id/apply-rules`; plus print, export, share where the screens already offer them.
16. **Contextual actions** [proposed]: open the linked document, copy number, view audit history, and row actions limited to what the status and permissions allow.
17. **Validation and constraints**: the server remains the authority (`zod` schemas in the route file, domain rules in `packages/domain`); the form mirrors them inline and never relaxes them. No written lifecycle applies.
18. **Approval behaviour**: No approval step in the routes.
19. **Permissions**: `banking.create`, `banking.edit`, `banking.view`. Menu entries and buttons are hidden without permission; the API refuses regardless.
20. **Business status states**: no lifecycle states are documented for this feature.
21. **Loading states**: skeleton rows or blocks of the final shape; actions disabled while saving to stop duplicate submission.
22. **Empty states**: say what the screen is for and offer the primary action (or, for a report, widen the period).
23. **Error states**: show the server's reason in plain words and what to do; keep the user's input; permission failures say which permission is missing.
24. **Responsive behaviour** [proposed]: desktop 1280 and above full layout; tablet 768 collapses the object-page tabs into a scroll row and the grid to key columns; mobile 375 uses stacked records with a bottom action bar. Tables never shrink; they scroll or restack.
25. **Keyboard interactions**: Tab order follows reading order; Enter submits the focused form; Esc closes dialogs; list rows open with Enter. Do not rebind browser shortcuts.
26. **Offline behaviour**: not declared in `OFFLINE_ACTIONS`, so treat as online only. The web app has no offline mode; the device app uses only the declared actions.
27. **API dependencies**: `GET /banking/accounts`, `PUT /banking/accounts/:id/profile`, `POST /banking/accounts/:id/statements`, `POST /banking/accounts/:id/feed/connect`, `POST /banking/accounts/:id/feed/disconnect`, `POST /banking/accounts/:id/feed/fetch`, `GET /banking/accounts/:id/lines`, `GET /banking/accounts/:id/open-entries` and 2 more. Contracts are not changed by the redesign.
28. **Existing backend limitations**: Routes, a screen and automated tests that call the routes are all present. Open items for this module are in `docs/product-discovery/10_IMPLEMENTATION_STATUS.md` section C.
29. **Related reports**: see the module's report column in the mega menu.
30. **Acceptance criteria**:
    - Every route listed under subfeatures still has an entry point.
    - Permissions match section 19 on screen and on the API; a user without them cannot reach the feature by menu, search or link.
    - Loading, empty, error and permission-denied states exist and were looked at.
    - No control is decorative: each one performs its action or is absent.
    - Keyboard-only use completes the primary task; focus is visible and returns sensibly after a dialog.
    - Layout verified at 1440, 1280, 768 and 375 px; tables scroll or restack.
    - Test evidence: `banking.test.ts` still pass.
    - Light and dark themes both checked; status is never shown by colour alone.
